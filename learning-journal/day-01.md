# Day 01 - Learning Journal

## Why am I pursuing AI research?

I am pursuing AI research because I want to move beyond using existing tools and contribute to developing solutions that solve meaningful, real-world problems. Throughout my career, I have been drawn to understanding complex systems, identifying patterns, and using data to support better decision-making. AI research provides an opportunity to combine those strengths with emerging technologies that have the potential to improve lives. My long-term goal is to help build AI systems that are not only technically capable but also trustworthy, transparent, and impactful. 

## Why does AI matter to me personally?

AI matters to me because I have seen firsthand how data and technology can improve outcomes in healthcare, government, and other mission-driven organizations. As both a healthcare professional and a data scientist, I understand the challenges of making decisions in complex, high-stakes environments. When applied responsibly, I believe AI has the potential to reduce inefficiencies, support better decision-making, and expand access to information. I want to be part of shaping the future.

## What skills do I currently have that will help me succeed?

My background has given me a unique combination of technical, analytical, and domain expertise. As a data scientist, I have experience working with Python, SQL, and R to analyze complex datasets, automate workflows, and communicate findings to both technical and non-technical audiences. My previous careers in healthcare and criminal investigations strengthened my skills in critical-thinking, evaluating evidence objectively, and solving ambiguous problems. I am comfortable learning new technologies, collaborating across disciplines, and breaking large challenges into manageable chunks, skills that I believe are essential for AI research.

## What skills do I need to develop?

To become an effective AI researcher, I need to deepen both my technical knowledge and my research methodology. My immediate priorities include strengthening my Python proficiency, learning PyTorch and modern deep learning frameworks, gaining hands-on experience with transformer architectures and large language models, and developing a stronger understanding of AI safety and model evaluation. I also want to improve my ability to design reproducible experiments, read and implement research papers, and communicate technical findings through clear documentation and academic research style writing.

## What do I hope to accomplish in the next 30 days?

Over the next 30 days, I will build a strong foundation in modern AI while creating a portfolio that reflects both my technical growth and my approach to problem-solving. My goal is to complete meaningful, well-documented projects that demonstrate practical experience with machine learning, large language models, and AI evaluation. In addition, I am developing the habits of an AI researcher by consistently reading technical papers, designing experiments, documenting my work, and reflecting on what I learn each day.

## Where do I want to be professionally one year from today?

One year from today, I will be working in a role where I am actively developing, evaluating, or researching AI systems that solve meaningful problems. Whether in an industry or a research-focused environment, I want to contribute to projects that advance trustworthy and responsible AI while continuing to grow as both a researcher and an engineer. I also want to have a portfolio of open, reproducible research that demonstrates not only what I have built, but how I think, learn, and approach complex technical challenges.
